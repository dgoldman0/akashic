# Picture Assets

Pictures for checking how Akashic shows real photographs.

## spice-market.qoi

A spice stall in an outdoor market in New Delhi, from the U.S. Food and Drug
Administration's "Foods and Flavors of India" series. As a work of the United
States federal government it is in the public domain; no attribution is
required.

- Source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Foods_and_Flavors_of_India_(8341561561).jpg>,
  from the FDA's Flickr stream.
- Original: JPEG, 3456 by 2592 pixels, 7,013,331 bytes, SHA-256
  `8f7f48853279dc17f9c2961308ce64c41adcf350b4bf31889246a1b482f5554a`.
- This copy: scaled to 2160 by 1620 pixels with Pillow 11.3's Lanczos filter,
  then written as opaque RGBA QOI by `local_testing/qoi_codec.py`; 8,144,442
  bytes.

2160 pixels is wider than File Explorer's preview at its largest on the
hosted physical viewer (195 cells of 11 pixels, 2145 pixels), so the copy the
terminal shows there is made at the area's own size and never enlarged. Its
strong colours and fine grain show both colour and sharpness.
